Location
We are located on the 2nd floor of the Sanders Law Firm Building and The Famous Steak House. To get into the building for your appointment, you'll enter from Kiowa Street. Look for the black awning that says 31 North Tejon, dial 0212 on the directory and press the phone symbol. We'll buzz you up.
Description
Located in the heart of Downtown Colorado Springs we are a full service salon hosting a variety of Independent and commission artists to meet your individual needs. We have a secure access location that our clients love. Specializing in the latest color trends, cuts and barbering styles. Our mission is to provide you with a friendly and personalized service. Our Independent professionals strive to exceed your expectations. We celebrate every woman, man and child in their real and authentic beauty, who believe in themselves and ready to step into the look they love.
